9. Advertising, Privacy Choices and Ad Reports

Where enabled, KOŁO initially uses Google AdMob interstitial advertising for eligible adults only. It does not show ads to a player whose age is unknown or under 18.

The initial experience uses no banner, rewarded or app-open ads and no mediation network. AdMob is configured with a maximum ad-content rating of G. Interstitials appear only at a natural break after a completed run, never during active play or over an age, consent, tracking, legal or purchase interface.

No interstitial appears during the first five completed lifetime runs. Later, KOŁO requires at least five completed runs and at least five minutes since the previous shown interstitial, and shows no more than three in one session. If an ad is not already loaded at the eligible break, KOŁO skips the opportunity and does not show it late.

Eligible adults can revisit required Google consent choices through Privacy Options. On Apple, ATT is requested only where KOŁO intends to use IDFA or tracking as Apple defines it. Refusing consent or ATT must not block gameplay.

Ads must be clearly identifiable, dismissible and not designed to cause accidental taps. KOŁO provides a Report an Ad route for inappropriate or age-inappropriate advertising. Reports should be made in good faith and should not include sensitive information unnecessarily.
