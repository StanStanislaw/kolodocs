1. Contact Support

Email hello@ostoya.games with KOŁO Support in the subject line.

Include the platform, device model, operating-system version, what you expected, what happened and the steps that reproduce the issue. For a purchase problem, include the product name and approximate purchase time, but do not send card details, a store-account password or a complete receipt containing unnecessary information.

2. Quick Troubleshooting

No sound or haptics

Check KOŁO Settings, silent mode, volume, Focus settings and system haptic settings. Fully close and reopen the app after changing an audio route.

A score or setting did not persist

Close and reopen the app once. KOŁO has no game account or cloud recovery system for ordinary local progress.

The age setting needs correction

Open Settings → Privacy & Age, adjust the selector and save. The exact age is not retained; only the resulting band and source are stored. Changing from 18+ to a protected band must stop eligible advertising and Analytics.

The game does not launch correctly

Restart the device, confirm that storage is available and update the operating system where practical. Reinstall only after noting that local progress may be removed.

3. Remove Ads Purchase and Restoration

Where available to an eligible adult, Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable entitlement processed by the applicable Platform Store. It removes all third-party advertising displayed inside KOŁO on that platform.

Apple

  1. Confirm that the App Store purchase completed and is not pending.
  2. Close and reopen KOŁO.
  3. Open KOŁO Settings and use Restore Purchases.
  4. Confirm that the device uses the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  5. Retry with a stable connection if the App Store cannot be reached.

Future Google Play release

  1. Confirm that Google Play reports the purchase as completed rather than pending.
  2. Confirm that Google Play uses the Google Account that owns the purchase.
  3. Close and reopen KOŁO.
  4. Use Restore Purchases or the equivalent entitlement-refresh action.
  5. Retry with a stable connection if Google Play cannot be reached.

Restoration recovers Remove Ads only. It does not restore local scores, settings or run history. Apple and Google Play purchases do not transfer between ecosystems.

4. Pending, Failed or Missing Purchases

A transaction can remain pending because of family or parental approval, payment verification, account restrictions, network conditions or store-service status. KOŁO does not unlock Remove Ads until the applicable store reports a valid completed and verified purchase.

On Apple, Ask to Buy or payment approval can delay completion. On a future Google Play release, delayed payment methods can leave a purchase pending until it changes to purchased and is processed correctly.

If the Platform Store shows a completed charge but KOŁO cannot restore the entitlement, contact us with the platform, approximate transaction date and only the non-sensitive information needed to identify the issue.

5. Refunds and Purchase History

Apple

Apple processes App Store refunds and purchase history. Use Apple’s Report a Problem service for an eligible App Store refund request.

Apple Report a Problem

Google Play

If KOŁO is later sold through Google Play, use Google Play’s refund workflow. Depending on the purchase, time elapsed and location, Google may direct you to the developer for support.

Request a Google Play refund

A refund, cancellation, reversal or revocation may remove Remove Ads. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

6. Privacy, Age and Local Data

KOŁO has no game account. Scores, settings, age-band choices and compliance records are stored locally. The exact age selected is not retained.

No advertising, Google UMP, Firebase Analytics or ATT request is permitted before age is known. Players under 18 receive no advertising, advertising Analytics or cross-app tracking. Eligible adults remain subject to consent and platform-permission choices.

Deleting the app generally removes local data, subject to operating-system behaviour and backups. Purchase records are controlled by the Platform Store and may remain available so a valid entitlement can be verified or restored.

7. Accessibility

KOŁO includes settings intended to support different play preferences. The exact system haptic, vibration, sound and accessibility behaviour may vary between supported devices.

Send accessibility feedback by email and include the platform, assistive technology or setting used and the exact screen where the problem occurs.

8. Advertising, Privacy Options and Report an Ad

KOŁO’s initial advertising plan is limited to Google AdMob interstitials for eligible adults. Players under 18 receive no ads. Eligible interstitials appear only after a completed run, never during gameplay, and are subject to the initial frequency limits described in the Terms.

Privacy Options

When Google UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, open KOŁO Settings → Privacy Options to review or change the available consent choices. On Apple devices, tracking permission can also be changed in iOS Settings. Refusing consent or ATT does not block gameplay.

Report an Ad

KOŁO provides a route to report an inappropriate or age-inappropriate advertisement. Use the in-app Report an Ad control or email hello@ostoya.games.

Include the approximate date and time, the screen where the ad appeared, why it was inappropriate and a screenshot if one is safely available. Do not tap the advertisement just to gather information. Do not send an advertising identifier, payment data, a store-account password or information about another person.

Why an adult may see an ad

Ad eligibility is based on the adult age band, Remove Ads ownership, consent status, platform permissions, run count, elapsed time and whether an interstitial was already loaded at a natural break. Personalised advertising is permitted only where the required consent and, on Apple, ATT permission allow it.
